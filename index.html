Chest equipment note: Your current floor-only dumbbell setup can train the pectoral muscles with floor presses and squeeze presses, but it cannot separately bias the upper chest like an incline press on an adjustable bench. No push-ups are included, as requested.
Weekly plan: Three non-consecutive days, alternating A and B (Week 1: A/B/A; Week 2: B/A/B). A day off between sessions gives the same muscles time to recover. Keep the first weeks easy while learning the movements.

Shoulders · all 3 heads

Front: shoulder press (B). Side: lateral raise (A). Rear: reverse fly (B). Chest presses also involve the front shoulder.

Forearms · balanced work

Flexors: wrist curl (A). Extensors: reverse wrist curl (B). Brachioradialis: hammer curl (B). Grip: farmer hold (A).

Chest · no push-ups

Dumbbell floor press (A) and squeeze floor press (B) train the chest. Keep wrists stacked and use a pain-free range.

Calves

Standing calf raise (A) and bent-knee calf raise (B) provide two calf-focused variations. Use stable support for balance.
